# StarCraft II race emblems

`zerg.png`, `protoss.png` and `terran.png` are Blizzard Entertainment's StarCraft II race emblems,
taken from the official site (starcraft2.blizzard.com, the selected-race icons) on 2026-10-27 and
scaled down to 128 pixels.
